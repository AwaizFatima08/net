The real pattern

200 questions in 3 hours: Mathematics 100, Physics 60 and English 40, following the NET-2025 onward weighting of 50 / 30 / 20 percent.

Timed like the day

A countdown, flags and a question map, no calculator and no negative marking, so you build the pace the paper needs: about 54 seconds a question.

Scores and explanations

Your score out of 200, marks per subject, the topics to work on, a merit-aggregate estimate, and a worked explanation for every question.

What you get

  • Subject-wise practice. Topic-by-topic question pools for Mathematics, Physics and English, plus Chemistry and Biology (NET-Applied Sciences) and Quantitative Mathematics (NET-Business), kept separate from the mocks.
  • Rules and guidelines. The five NET papers, eligibility, the 75 / 15 / 10 merit formula, series and fees, ACT/SAT route, and what to bring on test day.
  • Tips and formula sheets. A 12-week plan, time management for 200 questions, MCQ strategy without a calculator, English techniques, and Maths and Physics formula sheets.
  • Private and offline. No account. Your scores stay on your phone.

NET at a glance

  • Papers: NET-Engineering (Maths 50%, Physics 30%, English 20%), NET-Applied Sciences (Biology 50%, Chemistry 30%, English 20%), NET-Business & Social Sciences (Quantitative Maths 50%, English 50%), NET-Architecture (Design Aptitude 50%, Maths 30%, English 20%), NET-Natural Sciences (Maths 50%, English 50%).
  • Format: 200 four-option MCQs, 3 hours, 1 mark each, no negative marking. Computer-based in Islamabad and Quetta, paper-based in Karachi and Gilgit.
  • Merit: NET 75%, HSSC 15%, SSC 10%; at least 60% in both SSC and HSSC. Your best NET score in the cycle counts.
  • Series: up to four series a year, one attempt per series. Fee Rs 5,000 (USD 40) per test.

Facts checked on 9 November 2026. Always confirm dates, fees and rules on the official sites, nust.edu.pk and ugadmissions.nust.edu.pk.
