## 1. NET types (streams), programmes covered, and who may sit each

NUST defines the standard HSSC groups like this: Pre-Engineering = Maths, Physics, Chemistry; Pre-Medical = Biology, Physics, Chemistry; ICS = Maths, Physics, Computer Science. (WEIGHTS)

For NET-2027 there are **five NET papers**. (WEIGHTS)

| NET paper | Programmes it leads to | Who sits it (academic background) | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| **NET-Engineering** | All Engineering programmes and all Computing programmes (BS CS, BS Data Science, BS AI, BS Bioinformatics). The same score can also be used, with no extra fee and no second NET, for BS Robotics & AI, BS Bioinformatics, BS Mathematics, BS Physics, BS Chemistry and BS Food Science & Technology. | Pre-Engineering; ICS; Pre-Medical (see the notes below); Pre-Engg + additional Biology; Pre-Med + additional Maths; any HSSC with Maths as a major subject (Computing / Robotics & AI only) | WEIGHTS, MERIT |
| **NET-Applied Sciences** | BS Biotechnology, BS Environmental Science, BS Agriculture, BS Food Science & Technology, BS Bioinformatics, BS Chemistry, BS Physics | Pre-Medical, with or without additional Maths | WEIGHTS |
| **NET-Business Studies & Social Sciences** | BBA, BS Public Administration, BS Mass Communication, BS Economics, BS Psychology, BS Accounting & Finance, LLB, BS Tourism & Hospitality Management, BS Liberal Arts & Humanities, BS International Relations | HSSC with any subject combination. BS Accounting & Finance also needs Maths, Accounting & Finance or Accountancy. LLB also needs a valid HEC LAT score of at least 50%. | WEIGHTS, ELIG |
| **NET-Architecture** | B Architecture, B Industrial Design only | HSSC (and SSC) with Maths and Physics | WEIGHTS, ELIG |
| **NET-Natural Sciences** | BS Mathematics, BS Physics, BS Chemistry only | Candidates who meet the eligibility rules but are **outside** the standard streams (for example, Maths/Stats/CS or Physics/Chemistry combinations) | WEIGHTS |

Notes on Pre-Medical candidates:
- Pre-Medical students **without additional Maths** may sit NET-Engineering for Engineering programmes or BS Robotics & AI. Before admission they must clear NUST's online 8-week condensed Mathematics course. For NET-2027 the course must be finished by **25 Jul 2027** with at least **60%** to enter the Fall 2027 merit. Registration: https://connected.nust.edu.pk/course/view.php?id=186. This follows PEC regulation PEC/EAD/49-GB/004/2024 of 11 Jul 2024. (UPDATES, ELIG)
- Pre-Medical students without additional Maths who join a **Computing** programme must clear 6 credit hours of deficient Maths courses within one year. (UPDATES, ELIG, FAQ)

Note on ICS candidates: they may apply for all Engineering programmes but must clear Chemistry as a remedial course in the first semester. This follows PEC regulation PEC/EAD/46-VCCM/Regulations/2023 of 30 Mar 2023. (UPDATES, ELIG)

Other routes:
- **Medical (MBBS):** there is no NET for MBBS. NUST accepts only the NUMS MDCAT, with at least 55%. **BS Human Nutrition & Dietetics** accepts NET-Applied Sciences, NUMS MDCAT or National MDCAT (minimum 50%), or ACT (composite 25). BS Nursing is listed under "Other programmes". (BROCHURE)
- **DAE holders** may apply on open merit to the engineering programmes PEC has approved for each diploma. They need at least 60% in Matric and in the DAE. (https://nust.edu.pk/admissions/undergraduates/dae-eligibility-criteria/)

## 3. Syllabus by subject

**What NUST publishes officially:** no chapter-by-chapter syllabus for the MCQ subjects. NUST says only that:
- NET is "compiled from text books prescribed for HSSC courses"; candidates should consult all relevant textbooks of the various boards (SALIENT);
- it is based on SSC/HSSC (Part I and II) studies (FAQ);
- English follows the SAT pattern (FAQ);
- NUST provides no preparation material and endorses no academy (SALIENT, PORTAL "CAUTION").

The only official topic list is for **Design Aptitude** (SADA, summarised below). The other topic lists are *(secondary)* or are the standard FSc Part I/II textbook chapters (marked "FSc textbook"). Check them against the current provincial and federal (FBISE) textbooks before treating them as final.

### 3.1 Mathematics (NET-Engineering, NET-Architecture, NET-Natural Sciences): *(secondary: maqsad.io/ecat/nust-syllabus; matches the FSc Part I/II chapters)*
- Number systems and complex numbers
- Matrices and determinants
- Quadratic equations
- Sequences and series (AP, GP, HP)
- Permutations, combinations, probability
- Mathematical induction and binomial theorem
- Trigonometry (identities, graphs, solutions of triangles, inverse functions, trigonometric equations)
- Functions and graphs, limits and continuity
- Differential calculus (derivatives and their applications)
- Integral calculus (indefinite and definite integrals, area)
- Analytic geometry (straight lines, circles, conic sections)
- Vectors (2-D and 3-D, dot and cross product)
- Linear inequalities and linear programming (FSc textbook)
- Partial fractions, sets, functions and groups (FSc textbook)

Some prep sites also mention a few Matric-level questions *(secondary)*.

### 3.2 Physics (NET-Engineering): *(secondary: maqsad.io/ecat/nust-syllabus; FSc Part I/II chapters)*
- Measurements; vectors and equilibrium
- Motion and force (kinematics, dynamics)
- Work, energy, power
- Circular and rotational motion; gravitation
- Fluid dynamics
- Oscillations; waves and sound
- Physical optics; optical instruments
- Thermodynamics
- Electrostatics; current electricity
- Electromagnetism; electromagnetic induction; alternating current
- Physics of solids; electronics
- Modern physics: dawn of modern physics, atomic spectra, nuclear physics

### 3.3 Chemistry (NET-Applied Sciences only since NET-2025): FSc textbook headlines
- Part I: fundamental concepts (mole, stoichiometry); experimental techniques; gases; liquids and solids; atomic structure; chemical bonding; thermochemistry; chemical equilibrium; solutions; electrochemistry; reaction kinetics.
- Part II: periodic table and periodicity; s- and p-block elements; transition elements; organic chemistry basics; hydrocarbons; alkyl halides; alcohols, phenols, ethers; aldehydes and ketones; carboxylic acids; macromolecules; industrial and environmental chemistry.

Prep sites say NUST gives no official topic preference in Chemistry *(secondary: blog.maqsad.io)*.

### 3.4 Biology (NET-Applied Sciences): FSc textbook headlines
- Part I: biological molecules; enzymes; cell structure and function; variety of life (viruses, bacteria, protists, fungi); kingdom Plantae; kingdom Animalia; bioenergetics; nutrition; gaseous exchange; transport.
- Part II: homeostasis; support and movement; coordination and control; reproduction; growth and development; chromosomes and DNA; cell cycle; variation and genetics; biotechnology; evolution; ecosystem; environment.

### 3.5 English (all streams)
- Officially: "SAT pattern" (FAQ).
- Prep-site topic headlines *(secondary)*: vocabulary (synonyms, antonyms, word knowledge), analogies, sentence completion, grammar and sentence structure (error identification, sentence improvement), reading comprehension.

### 3.6 Quantitative Mathematics (NET-Business Studies & Social Sciences): *(secondary: blog.maqsad.io, 4 Jun 2026)*
- Arithmetic and basic quantitative reasoning (percentages, ratios, word problems)
- Algebra and functions
- Geometry and trigonometry
- Matrices
- Probability and statistics
- Limits and basic calculus

Earlier pages called this "Quantitative (Basic Mathematics)" (WEIGHTS via archive, Sep 2024).

### 3.7 Design Aptitude (NET-Architecture): **official** (SADA)
NUST lists five theme areas:
- **Design fundamentals and theory:** elements and principles of design, Gestalt, ratio and proportion systems, design vocabulary, colour theory, patterns in nature, analysing compositions.
- **Visual arts and techniques:** drawing and painting basics, shapes and geometry, the human figure, form-making (subtraction, addition, modelling, casting), materials (clay, wood, plaster, paper).
- **Design history and context:** iconic artists and designers, post-industrial movements (Arts & Crafts, Art Deco, Bauhaus, modernism, minimalism, postmodernism), local crafts, Islamic art and architecture, man and environment.
- **Problem-solving and visual communication:** non-verbal and diagrammatic reasoning, symbols and signage.
- **Contemporary and emerging practice:** studio culture and design process, notable Pakistani artists, architects and designers, emerging materials and technologies.

### 3.8 Computer Science and Intelligence
- **Not part of any NET-2027 paper** (WEIGHTS). They were last examined in NET-2024 (see section 8).
- The official demo still has a "With Computer Science" engineering sample (DEMO).

## 4. Merit formula, eligibility, seats

### 4.1 Aggregate (NET basis), official (MERIT)

| Component | Weight |
|---|---|
| NET score | **75%** |
| SSC / O Level / equivalent | **10%** |
| HSSC Part I / HSSC / A Level / equivalent / DAE | **15%** |

- O/A-Level candidates still in A-Level final year: their O-Level equivalence marks (IBCC) carry the 25% weight instead. (MERIT)
- The same 75/10/15 split was on the page in May 2024, so it is unchanged (web.archive.org snapshot 20240518 of MERIT).
- Example formula for the app (derived): aggregate = 0.75 × (NET/200 × 100) + 0.10 × SSC% + 0.15 × HSSC%.

### 4.2 Which NET score counts, per programme (MERIT)
- Engineering, Computing, BS Robotics & AI, Architecture and Business/Social Sciences/LLB all use the **best score** in the relevant NET.
- For BS Bioinformatics, BS Food Science & Technology, BS Chemistry and BS Physics (Pre-Engg/Pre-Med), the best score counts "even if appeared in both" NET-Engineering and NET-Applied Sciences.
- For candidates outside the standard streams, NET-Natural Sciences counts for BS Maths, BS Physics and BS Chemistry.

### 4.3 ACT / SAT routes (MERIT, SAT, UPDATES, PORTAL)
- **National seats:** ACT/SAT score 75% (replacing NET), SSC 10%, HSSC 15%. **International seats:** ACT/SAT 100%. The SSC/HSSC 60% minimum still applies.
- Minimum scores:
  - Engineering, Computing, Robotics & AI, Applied Sciences, Food Science, Architecture/Industrial Design: ACT Composite (Maths + English + Reading for tests from April 2025 on) of at least 25 **and** ACT Science of at least 25.
  - Natural Sciences: ACT STEM of at least 25.
  - Business/Social Sciences/LLB: SAT of at least 550 in each section, **or** ACT Composite of at least 25.
- Logistics: ACT superscore accepted. Scores are valid for 2 years. Institution codes: ACT 2915, SAT 2790.
- Dates: the ACT/SAT application runs 4 May to 25 Jul 2027 (DATES). The portal says registration starts 20 Apr 2027 (PORTAL); this conflicts with DATES. The last date for scores to reach NUST is **25 Jul 2027** (DATES, PORTAL).
- International candidates cannot apply for Aerospace or Avionics Engineering at CAE Risalpur (SAT, SALIENT). There are no ACT-based international seats at CAE (PORTAL).
- "A limited number of seats are reserved in all UG programmes" for ACT/SAT (BROCHURE). No seat numbers are published.

### 4.4 Eligibility (ELIG, SALIENT, INELIGIBILITY https://nust.edu.pk/admissions/undergraduates/ineligibility-criteria/)
- **At least 60% aggregate each in SSC and HSSC**, or equivalent (IBCC equivalence for O/A Level, IB, American diploma, etc.).
- FA/FSc candidates may register on their **HSSC Part I** result. Admission is confirmed on the full result with 60%. A Part-I score below 60% is allowed for registration, and those marks are used in the merit (FAQ).
- O/A-Level candidates register on their O-Level IBCC equivalence. The A-Level equivalence (60%) is needed before classes start.
- Ineligible: below 60% in SSC or HSSC; failed or absent in any HSSC Part I/II subject; no O-Level IBCC equivalence when applying; expelled from NUST.
- Candidates sitting a 2nd annual board exam or the Oct/Nov O/A sitting are not eligible for that session (SELECT).
- There is **no age limit**, and gap-year or repeat candidates are not penalised (FAQ).
- A CNIC, B-Form or NICOP is mandatory to register; foreign nationals use a passport (SALIENT).
- SSC (Science group) is required for Engineering and Applied Sciences (BROCHURE, ELIG).

### 4.5 Quotas and seats
- No quota table appears on the official UG pages read.
- NUST states a non-discriminatory, merit-only admission policy, with one intake a year (Fall) (SALIENT).
- The brochure gives a "4% UG student acceptance rate" (BROCHURE).

## 5. NET-2027 schedule, fees, attempts, results, centres

### 5.1 Schedule, official, labelled "Tentative" (DATES; same table on PORTAL)

| Series | Online registration | CBNET (Islamabad) | CBNET (Quetta) | PBNET (Karachi) | PBNET (Gilgit) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Series 1 | **4 Oct – 14 Nov 2026** | **17 Nov 2026 onwards** | – | – | – |
| Series 2 | Dec 2026 – Jan 2027 | Jan – Feb 2027 | Mar 2027 | Mar 2027 | – |
| Series 3 | Feb – Mar 2027 | Apr 2027 | – | – | – |
| Series 4 | Apr – May 2027 | Jun – Jul 2027 | Jul 2027 | Jul 2027 | Jul 2027 |

- Each series needs its own online form and fee (DATES, FEE).
- The PORTAL shows "NUST Entry Test (Series-1) Application Form, Last Date: 14 Nov 2026" (read 9 Nov 2026).
- The BROCHURE gives a coarser version: S1 registration Oct–Nov with CBNET Nov–Dec; S2 Dec–Jan, CBNET Feb, PBNET Mar; S3 Feb–Mar, CBNET Mar–Apr; S4 Apr–Jun, CBNET Jun–Jul, PBNET Jul.
- Merit lists come out tentatively in the **last week of July**. Candidates then get 1–2 days to edit their preferences (SELECT). Selection for all UG programmes starts in August (DATES; the page still says "August 2026"). Selection runs until about 2 weeks after classes begin (SELECT).
- Classes start Sep 2026 at most schools, Oct at MCS and Nov at CAE (DATES "Commencement 2026"; the 2027 dates are not yet posted).

### 5.2 Fees (FEE, SALIENT, BROCHURE)

| Category | Fee per NET (per series) |
|---|---|
| Pakistani / dual national | **Rs 5,000 or USD 40** |
| Foreign national (non-Pakistani) | **Rs 10,000 or USD 80** |
| ACT/SAT national seat | Rs 5,000 or USD 40 |
| ACT/SAT international seat | Rs 10,000 or USD 80 |

- The fee is non-refundable and non-transferable, and is valid only for the series paid for (FEE).
- Payment is by 1Link (17-digit 1Bill invoice number: bank app, ATM, Easypaisa, JazzCash; verified instantly) or by credit card (PROC, FAQ).
- First, second and third position holders in HSSC on any BISE are exempt from the fee (FAQ).
- The BROCHURE words it as "per exam per programme".

### 5.3 Attempts (SALIENT, PROC, PORTAL)
- A candidate may sit NET **more than once**: one attempt per series for a given discipline, so at most 4 per cycle (derived).
- The **best score** is used for merit.
- The NET result is valid **for the current admission cycle only**.
- CBNET candidates choose their own test date and session. PBNET dates are allotted first come, first served.

### 5.4 Results (FAQ, re-checking page https://nust.edu.pk/admissions/undergraduates/re-checking-of-net-papers/)
- **CBNET results come within 24 hours. PBNET results come in 5–7 working days.** They are viewed by roll number on the portal (https://ugadmissions.nust.edu.pk/result/default.aspx; this returned 404 on 9 Nov 2026, presumably because it opens only when results are out).
- Re-checking is for **PBNET only**: apply within 5 days of the result, with Rs 500 by bank draft or pay order. It checks for unmarked answers and totalling errors.

### 5.5 Centres (SALIENT, FAQ, guide map https://nust.edu.pk/admissions/undergraduates/guide-map-for-test-venue/)
- **Islamabad (CBNET):** NUST Exam Hall, entry through Gate 4, NUST H-12 campus, opposite the Srinagar (Kashmir) Highway.
- **Quetta (CBNET):** NUST Balochistan Campus (NBC).
- **Karachi and Gilgit (PBNET):** the venue is printed on the admit card.
- **No overseas centres.** Expatriates sit NET in Pakistan or use ACT/SAT.

## 6. Test-day rules

What NUST officially says (PROC, FAQ, SALIENT):
- CBNET candidates are given a roll number, test centre, session, login ID and password. PBNET candidates are emailed to print an admit card showing the exact time and centre.
- Bring the **printed admit card** and **one original photo ID**: CNIC, passport, Family Registration Certificate, or Matric/FSc certificate with photograph. **The admit card is kept at the test centre** for verification.
- A missed session is not rescheduled. "Extreme emergency" requests may be considered case by case, if space allows (FAQ).
- CBNET runs in **two sessions daily** on different days. PBNET is one day (FAQ).
- The CBNET interface, from the official demo (DEMO, interface only, no items copied):
  - login screen (user ID and password);
  - subject sections;
  - "Question No. x of N" with "Marks: 1";
  - four radio-button options;
  - start time and a countdown of minutes remaining;
  - a "Click here to FINISH your test" button.
  - NUST says the CBT is "easy to attempt with minimal practice" (SALIENT).

Not found on any official page read: reporting time (it is printed on the admit card), the list of prohibited items, calculators, rough sheets, dress code. Prep sites commonly say calculators and phones are not allowed *(secondary)*. See section 9.

## 9. Open questions / could not confirm

1. **Per-subject MCQ counts.** NUST gives only percentages and "200 MCQs". The counts (100/60/40, etc.) are derived. Prep sites agree for Engineering and Business.
2. **Is every stream really 200 MCQs in 180 min?** The FAQ and brochure say so generically. No stream-specific statement was found, notably for **Design Aptitude**: it is unclear whether that section is all MCQ or includes drawing.
3. **Calculators, rough sheets, prohibited items, reporting time, dress code.** None of these is on any official page read. They are probably on the admit card or the CBNET instruction screen. Calculators are not allowed according to most prep sites *(secondary, and one site disagrees)*. Confirm with a real admit card or ugadmissions@nust.edu.pk / +92-51-90856878.
4. **Official topic syllabus for Maths, Physics, Chemistry, Biology, English and Quant.** NUST publishes none. Topic lists in section 3 come from prep sites or FSc textbook chapters. Verify against the current FBISE and Punjab textbooks (the curricula were revised in 2023–25).
5. **Exact Series 2–4 dates** are given only as months ("Tentative"). They need re-checking when each series opens.
6. **ACT/SAT registration start.** DATES says 4 May 2027, the PORTAL says 20 Apr 2027.
7. **Results URL** (https://ugadmissions.nust.edu.pk/result/default.aspx) returned 404 on 9 Nov 2026. It is probably reactivated during series.
8. **The demo test is outdated** (pre-2025 subjects). There is no sign NUST will update it.
9. **The brochure file name says "2026"** but its content covers Fall 2027 (QS 2027 rank, BS Robotics & AI, BS IR). There may be a newer one not yet linked.
10. **Overseas centres:** none on official pages for 2024–2027. Older prep material mentioning Gulf centres should be treated as outdated.
11. **Quota/reserved-seat numbers** (ACT/SAT seat counts, any regional or special quotas) are not published on the UG pages read.
12. **Negative marking and options** come from the FAQ and brochure, not a dedicated NET rules page. They are consistent across official sources.
